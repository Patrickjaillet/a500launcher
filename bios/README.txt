Put your Amiga 500 Kickstart ROM here (a .rom or .bin file).

Kickstart 1.2 (33.180) or 1.3 (34.005) is recommended.
The application detects it automatically at startup when the
"Kickstart ROM" setting is empty.

Use only a ROM you own legally (a dump of your own Amiga,
Cloanto Amiga Forever, etc.).
